How I work

A process built to stay grounded in real problems, from first questions to shipped screens.

01Understand

Getting clear on the problem, the people affected by it, and what success actually looks like before any screens are drawn.

02Explore

Sketching a wide range of directions and possible flows without committing early, to avoid designing the first idea instead of the best one.

03Structure

Turning exploration into information architecture and user flows that make the product easy to navigate.

04Design

Building the interface itself i.e layout, type, colour, and components with attention to detail and consistency.

05Test

Checking assumptions against real feedback where possible, and watching for friction points before launch.

06Refine

Polishing details, tightening interactions, and iterating after launch as new information comes in.
