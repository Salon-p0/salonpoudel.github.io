About

A designer who likes to understand how things work.

I’m a UI/UX designer focused on creating digital experiences that are simple, thoughtful, and easy to use. My work spans product design, web design, WordPress, and visual branding, with a strong focus on turning ideas into clear and engaging digital experiences.

I enjoy working across different parts of the design process, from understanding problems and structuring user flows to designing interfaces and bringing websites to life. Alongside UI/UX, I also work on social media and visual design, which has helped me develop a broader understanding of how design connects with communication and branding.

I’m also exploring digital marketing as a natural extension of my design practice, particularly content, SEO, social media, and email marketing. I’m interested in how good design and marketing can work together to create experiences that look good, communicate clearly, and drive action. I mainly use Figma for UI/UX and product design, WordPress and Elementor for web projects, and Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, XD, and Canva for visual and social design.
